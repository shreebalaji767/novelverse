# **THE WRONG HEIRESS**

## **Chapter 5 — The Heiress Who Wanted Her Old Life Back**

**Monday — 8:20 a.m.**

Aria had three problems.

The first was that her kitchen tap had started leaking.

The second was that the internet technician had promised to arrive between eight and nine, which apparently meant sometime before the next century.

The third was that her phone had received seventeen messages from people she barely knew.

She ignored all seventeen and examined the tap.

A drop of water fell into the sink.

Then another.

Then another.

Aria stared at it.

"You're not even paying rent."

She tightened the handle.

The tap continued leaking.

"Wonderful."

Her phone rang.

Marcus Vale.

Aria answered while searching for a screwdriver.

"Good morning."

"Good morning, Miss Sterling. I hope you're well."

"I'm negotiating with a tap."

Marcus paused.

"Is everything all right?"

"Not particularly. The tap is winning."

"Would you like me to arrange someone to repair it?"

Aria stopped searching.

"No."

"Are you certain?"

"Yes. I can call the landlord."

"Of course."

A pause.

"How was your visit yesterday?"

"Good. Your family is nice."

Marcus allowed himself a small smile.

"I'm glad."

"Your grandfather is funny."

"I'll tell him."

"Please don't. He'll probably start making jokes professionally."

Marcus almost laughed.

Then he remembered why he had called.

"Miss Sterling, there is something I should inform you about."

Aria found the screwdriver.

"What is it?"

"Victoria Sterling visited the estate yesterday."

Aria stopped.

"Victoria?"

"Yes. She was not permitted to enter because she did not have an appointment."

"Did she want to meet me?"

"She wanted to meet the Vesper family."

Aria leaned against the counter.

"Why?"

"That is unclear."

Aria considered it.

"She's probably curious."

"Perhaps."

"Did she cause trouble?"

"Nothing serious."

"Then leave it."

Marcus was silent for a moment.

"Are you sure?"

"Yes. I don't want to get involved in whatever is happening at the Sterling mansion."

"Understood."

"Goodbye, Marcus."

"Goodbye, Miss Sterling."

The call ended.

Aria returned to the tap.

It was still leaking.

"At least you're consistent."

**9:05 a.m. — Vesper Tower**

Victoria Sterling had arrived twenty-five minutes early.

She was wearing a tailored white suit and carrying a designer handbag.

The reception area was enormous, with polished stone floors and a wall displaying the Vesper Global emblem.

She looked around approvingly.

This was more like it.

A receptionist approached.

"Good morning. May I help you?"

"I'm Victoria Sterling. I have an appointment with Evelyn Ashford."

"Certainly, Miss Sterling. Please wait."

Victoria sat down.

She examined the building.

The reception staff were efficient. Security was discreet. Everyone appeared busy.

She liked it.

A company of this size would understand the importance of status.

After several minutes, a woman approached.

"Miss Sterling?"

Victoria stood.

"Yes."

"I'm Evelyn Ashford."

Victoria smiled.

"Finally. I've been looking forward to meeting you."

Evelyn offered her hand.

"Please come with me."

They entered a private conference room.

A folder was already waiting on the table.

Victoria sat.

Evelyn took the opposite chair.

"Thank you for coming."

"Of course. I understand the Vesper family has questions about the hospital incident."

"Yes."

Victoria smiled.

"I'm happy to cooperate."

Evelyn opened the folder.

"First, I need to clarify something. The Vesper family has confirmed that Aria Sterling is their biological granddaughter."

Victoria's smile faded.

"I know."

"She was born to Lucian and Helena Vesper."

"Yes."

"Her identity has been verified by three independent laboratories."

Victoria shifted in her chair.

"Then what does that have to do with me?"

Evelyn looked at her.

"You were raised by the Sterling family. Your biological identity has also been established."

Victoria nodded.

"Exactly."

"Your biological parents are Richard and Eleanor Sterling."

Victoria's expression brightened slightly.

"Yes."

Evelyn closed the folder.

"That is the matter we wished to clarify."

Victoria stared at her.

"That's it?"

"Regarding your identity, yes."

Victoria waited.

Then she smiled again.

"Well, I'm sure the Vesper family would still like to meet me."

Evelyn remained polite.

"That decision belongs to the family."

"Of course. Perhaps you could arrange a meeting?"

"I can communicate your request."

Victoria leaned forward.

"Do you know who my father is?"

"Mr. Richard Sterling."

"He's the chairman of Sterling Holdings."

"I'm aware."

"Our family has extensive business interests."

Evelyn nodded.

"Yes."

Victoria smiled.

"Then I'm sure you understand why a relationship between our families could be beneficial."

Evelyn looked at her for a moment.

"Miss Sterling, this meeting is not a business negotiation."

Victoria's smile stiffened.

"Naturally."

Evelyn stood.

"Thank you for your time. Our legal department will contact you if any further information is required."

Victoria remained seated.

"You're dismissing me?"

"The meeting is complete."

Victoria slowly stood.

"Very well."

She picked up her handbag.

At the door, she turned.

"Please tell the Vespers that I would be delighted to meet them."

"I will pass on your message."

Victoria left.

Evelyn waited until the door closed.

Then she looked at the folder.

"Interesting."

She made a note.

**Victoria Sterling — requested personal meeting with Vesper family.**

Then she returned to work.

**10:12 a.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria returned home in a terrible mood.

Richard was reading a financial report when she entered.

"How did it go?"

"Disappointing."

"Why?"

"They treated me like an ordinary visitor."

Richard put down the report.

"What did you expect?"

Victoria looked offended.

"I expected some courtesy."

"Did they explain the hospital records?"

"Yes."

"And?"

"They confirmed Aria is their granddaughter."

Richard nodded.

"Then the meeting served its purpose."

Victoria stared at him.

"Father, don't you think this is strange?"

"What?"

"They are treating Aria as if she's important."

Richard frowned.

"She is their biological granddaughter."

"But I was raised by the Sterling family. Surely that should count for something."

"It counts to us."

Victoria looked at him.

"Then why aren't the Vespers interested in me?"

Richard sighed.

"Because you're not their relative."

Victoria's face tightened.

"That doesn't mean they have to ignore me."

"They haven't ignored you. They met with you."

"An office meeting."

Richard returned to his report.

"Victoria, I have work to do."

She stood.

For several seconds, she looked at him.

Then she turned and left.

**11:30 a.m. — Vesper Estate**

Helena was looking at a photograph.

It was one of the few pictures Marcus had obtained from Aria's school records.

Aria was standing beside a group of classmates, smiling at the camera.

Helena touched the edge of the photograph.

"She looks happy."

Isolde sat beside her.

"That is good."

"She must have had a life without us."

"Yes."

"Friends. School. People she trusts."

Isolde nodded.

"She has a life of her own. We must respect that."

Helena looked down.

"I want to know everything about her."

"You will, eventually."

A knock sounded.

Marcus entered.

"Madam. Chairman."

Octavian looked up from his newspaper.

"What is it?"

"Miss Aria has not requested any further arrangements. However, she did mention that her apartment requires a minor repair."

Helena immediately looked concerned.

"What repair?"

"A leaking kitchen tap."

Octavian lowered his newspaper.

"Arrange a repair."

Marcus nodded.

"Certainly."

"Use a reputable company."

"Yes, Chairman."

"And don't charge her."

Marcus paused.

"Of course."

Helena smiled.

"Please make sure they don't disturb her."

Marcus made a note.

"Understood."

Octavian returned to his newspaper.

After a moment, he spoke again.

"Marcus."

"Yes, Chairman?"

"Do not send an army to repair a tap."

Marcus looked at him.

"I wasn't planning to, sir."

"Good."

**12:05 p.m. — Aria's Apartment**

The doorbell rang.

Aria opened the door.

A man in a work uniform stood outside.

"Good afternoon. Plumbing repair."

She blinked.

"I didn't call anyone."

The man checked his tablet.

"Apartment 304?"

"Yes."

"Repair request for the kitchen tap."

Aria stared.

"Who sent you?"

"Vesper Services."

She closed her eyes.

Of course.

She stepped aside.

"Come in."

The plumber examined the tap.

"Simple washer problem."

"Can you fix it?"

"Yes, madam."

"How much?"

"Already covered."

Aria looked at him.

"By whom?"

"Vesper Services."

She sighed.

"Of course."

The plumber worked for several minutes.

The tap stopped leaking.

He packed his tools.

"All done."

"Thank you."

He handed her a receipt.

At the bottom was a note.

**Service covered by Vesper Family Office.**

Aria stared at it.

Then she took out her phone.

**Aria:** Marcus.

The reply arrived almost immediately.

**Marcus:** Yes, Miss Sterling?

**Aria:** Did you send a plumber?

**Marcus:** Yes. We were informed that your kitchen tap required repair.

**Aria:** I was going to call the landlord.

**Marcus:** I apologise if this was inconvenient.

Aria looked at the now-functioning tap.

**Aria:** It isn't inconvenient. But please ask before arranging things.

**Marcus:** Understood. I will ensure that future arrangements are made only with your approval.

**Aria:** Thank you.

She put her phone down.

Then she looked at the tap.

"Well. At least someone here is useful."

**1:20 p.m. — Vesper Estate, Security Office**

Damian was reviewing the previous day's security report when an officer entered.

"Sir."

"What is it?"

"We have completed the preliminary review of the hospital incident."

Damian looked up.

"Any new findings?"

"Yes. The original hospital's records show that one security access log was altered shortly after the births."

"By whom?"

"We're still identifying the account owner. The credentials belonged to a temporary hospital administrator."

Damian's expression hardened.

"Find the administrator."

"We're trying. The person left the hospital shortly after the incident."

"Name?"

"Daniel Mercer."

Damian opened the file.

"Where is he now?"

"Unknown. His last recorded address is outdated."

"Trace his employment history. Financial records. Travel records. Anyone who contacted him."

"Understood."

Damian looked at the hospital report again.

A deliberate switch.

An altered access log.

A missing administrator.

This was not a simple mistake.

He picked up his phone.

"Marcus."

"Yes?"

"I need to brief the Chairman."

"Send me the report."

"There's something else."

"What?"

"Someone had access to the hospital's security system using credentials that should have been disabled."

Marcus went silent.

"How old are the credentials?"

"Created six days before the births."

Marcus looked at his screen.

"Send everything."

Damian ended the call.

He stared at the report.

Someone had planned the switch.

And whoever had done it had known exactly where to find the two babies.

**3:10 p.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria was in the garden with Eleanor.

She had been quiet for most of the afternoon.

Eleanor noticed.

"Are you still upset about this morning?"

Victoria sighed.

"I simply don't understand why everyone is so interested in Aria."

Eleanor looked at her.

"She's the Vespers' granddaughter."

"But she doesn't even know them."

"That doesn't change her identity."

Victoria looked away.

"She was raised here."

Eleanor nodded.

"Yes."

"She had everything."

Eleanor did not answer.

Victoria continued.

"She had your attention. Father's attention. The house. The staff. The family name."

"Victoria..."

"And now she's suddenly important to another family."

Eleanor gently placed her hand over Victoria's.

"You don't need to compete with her."

Victoria looked at her mother.

"I'm not competing."

Eleanor said nothing.

Victoria smiled.

"I simply want everyone to understand that I belong here."

Eleanor squeezed her hand.

"You do."

Victoria nodded.

But the answer did not satisfy her.

Not completely.

**5:45 p.m. — Vesper Estate**

The family gathered in Octavian's study.

Damian placed the preliminary investigation report on the desk.

"Someone deliberately altered the hospital access records."

Octavian read the first page.

Lucian stood beside him.

"Was it connected to the security arrangements?"

"Yes," Damian replied. "The credentials belonged to a temporary administrator. They were created six days before the births and disabled shortly after."

Maximilian frowned.

"Who authorised them?"

"That is still being investigated."

Cassian leaned forward.

"Could someone inside the hospital have done it?"

"Certainly."

"Could someone outside have arranged it?"

"Also possible."

Felix examined the digital records.

"The access logs were modified manually. Whoever did it knew the system."

Damian nodded.

"That's our assessment."

Octavian closed the report.

"Keep this investigation restricted."

Lucian looked at him.

"Do you think someone is still involved?"

"I think we have only found the first person who touched the records."

Silence followed.

Helena looked toward the photograph on the desk.

"Who would do something like this to a newborn child?"

Octavian's expression hardened.

"Someone who believed they had something to gain."

He turned toward Damian.

"Find Daniel Mercer."

"Yes, Chairman."

"And find out who paid him."

Damian nodded.

Octavian looked around the room.

"No one approaches Aria with these findings until we have something reliable. She has had enough upheaval."

Lucian agreed.

"She deserves the truth, not speculation."

**8:32 p.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria was sitting on the floor, assembling a small bookshelf.

The instructions were confusing.

The screws were too small.

And one wooden panel appeared to have been manufactured by someone who had never seen a bookshelf.

She examined the diagram.

Then the panel.

Then the diagram again.

"That cannot possibly go there."

Her phone rang.

A message from Marcus.

**Marcus:** Miss Sterling, I wanted to confirm that the repair was completed satisfactorily.

**Aria:** Yes. The tap works. Thank you.

**Marcus:** I'm glad.

**Aria:** Please thank whoever arranged it.

**Marcus:** I will.

Aria put her phone down.

She finally fitted the wooden panel into place.

The bookshelf stood.

Crooked.

But standing.

She stepped back and admired it.

"Perfect."

One second later, the top shelf tilted.

Three books fell onto the floor.

Aria stared.

Then she sighed.

"Tomorrow, I'm buying a hammer."

**Vesper Estate — 9:10 p.m.**

Octavian stood alone in his private study.

The old hospital photograph was open on his desk.

Two babies.

Two bassinets.

Two names.

One missing granddaughter.

He looked at the report Damian had delivered.

The altered security credentials had been created six days before the births.

That meant someone had known the babies would be delivered at that hospital.

Someone had known their identities.

Someone had arranged access.

Octavian picked up the telephone.

"Marcus."

"Yes, Chairman?"

"Find out who requested the temporary administrator's credentials."

"We're already investigating."

"Go further back. Find the person who authorised the request."

"Understood."

Octavian looked at the photograph again.

"And Marcus?"

"Sir?"

"Until we know who did this, Aria's safety comes first."

"Yes, Chairman."

The call ended.

Octavian remained beside the desk.

For twenty years, he had believed that finding his granddaughter would end the worst chapter of his life.

Now he understood something.

Finding her had only revealed how much of the truth was still missing.

**End of Chapter 5**
