# **THE WRONG HEIRESS**

## **Chapter 42 — The Inheritance Nobody Wanted**

**Thursday — 8:00 a.m.**

The Vesper family had a problem.

It was not a financial problem.

It was not a security problem.

It was not even a legal problem, although several lawyers had already been summoned.

It was a breakfast problem.

Octavian had requested that Aria and Victoria attend a family meeting at nine.

At eight, Helena discovered that the kitchen staff had prepared breakfast for twenty-three people.

There were only twelve family members expected.

“Why are there twenty-three place settings?” she asked Marcus.

“Mr. Vesper requested that the legal representatives attend.”

“How many legal representatives?”

“Eleven.”

Helena stared at him.

“Eleven lawyers?”

“Yes.”

“For breakfast?”

Marcus considered the question.

“I believe they will also be attending the meeting.”

Helena looked at the enormous dining table.

“Do they all eat?”

Marcus paused.

“I haven't checked.”

**9:05 a.m. — Vesper Estate**

The lawyers had arrived.

They were seated in a separate room with the original succession instrument, the trust documents, and the recovered allocation records.

Aria and Victoria sat together.

Neither looked particularly interested.

Octavian stood at the head of the table.

“The document we recovered establishes a condition for the inheritance. However, its legal meaning requires clarification.”

One of the senior lawyers, Mr. Harrington, opened the original instrument.

“The condition does not mean the two descendants must choose one another as successors. It means neither can be appointed without the independent consent of both.”

Victoria frowned.

“Then what exactly is the inheritance?”

Mr. Harrington turned a page.

“The Vesper Legacy Trust.”

Cassian raised an eyebrow.

“Not the entire dynasty?”

“No. The trust holds a separate collection of assets, voting rights, and controlling interests established by an earlier generation.”

Maximilian leaned forward.

“How substantial?”

The lawyer placed a document on the table.

The figures were confidential, but the summary was clear.

The trust controlled significant interests across several international holdings, including assets that had never been consolidated into Vesper Global.

It was enormous.

But it was not the Vesper family's entire empire.

Victoria looked at the document.

“So this is why someone moved us around?”

“Possibly,” Mr. Harrington said. “The original instrument was designed to prevent the trust from being claimed through a single family's influence.”

Aria looked at him.

“Then why does it require both of us?”

“Because the trust's creator believed that the two descendants had to verify the original family allocation together. Neither could claim the inheritance by presenting only their own identity.”

Victoria folded her arms.

“So one of us could not simply walk in and take everything.”

“Correct.”

“And neither family could appoint a successor without the other girl's consent?”

“Correct.”

Aria nodded.

“That part makes sense.”

Everyone looked at her.

She shrugged.

“It's still a ridiculous amount of paperwork.”

**10:20 a.m.**

Mr. Harrington continued.

“The original succession instrument contains an additional provision. The trust cannot be transferred to either descendant until the circumstances surrounding their separation have been investigated.”

Octavian looked at him.

“Who has authority to determine whether that condition has been satisfied?”

“The independent trustees.”

“Who appointed them?”

“The original trust creator.”

“Names?”

The lawyer opened another document.

There were three trustees.

Two had died.

The third remained alive.

**Lord Adrian Blackwood.**

Marcus immediately checked the name.

“He is currently chairman of Blackwood Private Capital.”

Cassian looked at the screen.

“An independent trustee who happens to control a financial institution with interests across several Vesper sectors.”

“His appointment predates those business interests,” Mr. Harrington explained.

Octavian's expression darkened.

“Contact him.”

**11:40 a.m. — Blackwood Private Capital**

Adrian Blackwood received the call personally.

He listened to Marcus's request.

Then he asked one question.

“Have both descendants been identified?”

“Yes.”

“Have they both been informed of the succession condition?”

“Yes.”

“Have they accepted the inheritance?”

Marcus glanced at Aria.

“No.”

Blackwood gave a short laugh.

“Then nothing has changed.”

Marcus frowned.

“Excuse me?”

“The trust has remained inactive for twenty years. It will remain inactive until both descendants independently consent to proceed.”

“Can you explain the original arrangement?”

“No.”

“Why?”

“Because the trust agreement prohibits disclosure of the protected succession records before a formal joint verification.”

Marcus looked at Octavian.

Blackwood continued.

“However, I can tell you something important. The original allocation was not changed to give one child an advantage.”

“Then why was it changed?”

“Because someone believed the original allocation would expose both children to a danger.”

Marcus remained silent.

Blackwood added:

“And whoever made that decision did not have the authority to change the children's placements.”

The call ended.

**1:15 p.m. — Vesper Estate**

The family reviewed Blackwood's statement.

Damian was the first to speak.

“He knows more than he is saying.”

“That's likely,” Maximilian replied.

Felix opened the recovered documents.

“The trust's original records show that the allocation reversal was submitted before the births. But the custody changes happened afterward.”

Cassian leaned forward.

“So the person who reversed the allocation and the people who carried out the transfers may not have had the same objective.”

“Exactly,” Felix said.

Lucian looked at the records.

“Someone changed the intended destinations. Someone else arranged the hospital handovers. Someone else redirected Victoria from the Sterling estate.”

Aria looked at the timeline.

“And Jonathan took Victoria away from the estate.”

“Yes,” Damian said.

“Then there are several decisions, not one.”

Marcus nodded.

“That is the most consistent interpretation of the evidence.”

Octavian looked at the recovered instruction.

“Find the person who ordered the first reversal.”

**3:00 p.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria was reading the trust agreement when Richard entered.

“You should be careful,” he said.

She didn't look up.

“About what?”

“Getting involved in an inheritance dispute.”

“I'm already involved.”

“You don't understand the people who control these assets.”

Victoria closed the document.

“Then explain them.”

Richard hesitated.

“I cannot.”

“Because you don't know?”

He looked at her.

“Yes.”

Victoria nodded.

“That's honest, at least.”

Richard sat opposite her.

“I have spent years believing that money and influence could protect this family.”

“And?”

“And I was wrong.”

Victoria studied him.

“Then stop trying to protect me by keeping me uninformed.”

Richard lowered his eyes.

“I will try.”

Victoria returned to the agreement.

“Good. Because I'm not signing anything.”

**5:20 p.m. — Vesper Estate**

Aria found Isolde in the garden.

Her grandmother was reading a book.

“Can I ask you something?”

“Of course.”

“Did you know about the inheritance?”

Isolde closed the book.

“I knew Elias had created a private trust. I did not know he had connected it to the succession of two children.”

“Did you know he was worried about someone?”

“Yes.”

Aria sat down.

“Who?”

“Elias never told me.”

“Did you ask?”

“Many times.”

“And?”

“He said that if he explained his concerns before he had proof, he would endanger the people he was trying to protect.”

Aria considered that.

“Did he ever mention the Sterling family?”

“No.”

“Did he mention Richard?”

“No.”

“Did he mention anyone who might have arranged the switch?”

Isolde shook her head.

Aria looked toward the garden path.

“Then we're still missing the important part.”

“Yes.”

Isolde reached over and gently touched her hand.

“Whatever the trust contains, you do not owe it your life.”

Aria smiled faintly.

“That's reassuring.”

**7:10 p.m. — Vesper Tower**

Felix received a message from the independent trustee.

It contained one attachment.

A scanned page from the original trust register.

The document was titled:

**PROTECTED SUCCESSION — INCIDENT REPORT**

It had been created two weeks before the births.

The first paragraph stated:

*An attempt has been made to obtain the identities of the two expected descendants. The source of the inquiry is unknown.*

The second paragraph was more alarming.

*The inquiry originated from within the Vesper administrative structure.*

Maximilian read it twice.

“Someone inside the family knew about the children before they were born.”

Lucian looked at Octavian.

“Who had access to the expected births?”

“The immediate family, the medical team, and a limited number of administrative officers.”

Felix scrolled down.

There was another line.

*The inquiry was made using the credentials of a deceased family officer.*

Damian looked at the screen.

“Who?”

Felix opened the attached reference.

**MARGARET ELLISON.**

Cassian leaned back.

“She was already dead?”

“According to the official record, yes,” Felix replied.

Octavian looked at the document.

“Then someone was using her credentials before the children were born.”

Marcus received another message.

He read it.

Then he looked at Octavian.

“The trustee has agreed to meet us.”

“When?”

“Tomorrow morning.”

“Where?”

Marcus checked the location.

“Blackwood House.”

**10:55 p.m. — Blackwood House**

Adrian Blackwood sat alone in his study.

He opened a hidden compartment in his desk.

Inside was an old photograph.

It showed three men standing outside a private office.

Elias Vesper.

Octavian Vesper.

And a third man.

The third man's face was partly obscured by the angle of the photograph.

Blackwood turned it over.

A handwritten note was visible.

*The succession cannot be allowed to proceed while he remains alive.*

Blackwood placed the photograph beside the trust agreement.

Then he opened a small notebook.

The final entry was dated twenty-two years earlier.

**The original inquiry was made by the man who was supposed to protect the children.**

Blackwood closed the notebook.

He looked at the clock.

Tomorrow, the Vespers would finally learn who had initiated the investigation into the unborn children.

And why Elias had considered that person dangerous.
