# **THE WRONG HEIRESS**

## **Chapter 3 — Four Brothers and One Very Bad Plan**

**Vesper Estate — 8:15 a.m.**

Maximilian Vesper had a problem.

Actually, he had several.

The first was that his grandfather had ordered him to cancel an important overseas meeting.

The second was that his younger brothers had apparently forgotten how to behave like adults.

The third was that all three were currently arguing over a single question.

Who would meet their sister first?

"I should go," Cassian declared.

Maximilian didn't look up from his laptop.

"No."

"Why?"

"Because you turn every conversation into a negotiation."

"That's called communication."

"That's called being unbearable."

Cassian placed a hand over his chest.

"I am wounded."

Damian sat opposite them, reviewing a security report.

"Neither of you should go."

Cassian turned.

"Why not?"

"Because you're both making this unnecessarily complicated."

Felix, who had been silently typing on his tablet, raised his head.

"I could go."

All three brothers looked at him.

Felix continued.

"I've prepared a presentation explaining the family structure, our corporate holdings, the security arrangements, and the genetic verification process."

Maximilian closed his eyes.

"How long is it?"

"One hundred and eighty-six slides."

Silence.

Cassian slowly turned toward Damian.

"I think we have found the most dangerous person in this family."

Felix frowned.

"It's comprehensive."

"It's a hostage situation with slides," Cassian replied.

"I also prepared a shorter version."

"How many slides?"

"Seventy-two."

Maximilian shut his laptop.

"Nobody is giving her a presentation."

Felix looked genuinely disappointed.

"But she needs to understand—"

"She needs to meet her family, Felix. Not attend a corporate induction."

Damian put down his report.

"Grandfather has already said we should wait for her decision."

Cassian leaned back.

"Then we wait."

Maximilian looked at him suspiciously.

"Why are you agreeing so easily?"

"Because I have an alternative."

"No."

"You haven't heard it."

"I know you."

Cassian smiled.

"An informal lunch."

Damian shook his head.

"Absolutely not."

"Why?"

"Because you will invite her somewhere expensive, order everything on the menu, and turn the meeting into a performance."

Cassian considered this.

"That is an unfair description."

"Is it inaccurate?"

Cassian said nothing.

Felix raised his hand again.

"Could we invite her to the technology division?"

"No," all three answered.

**8:42 a.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria was trying to fix a kitchen drawer.

The drawer had become stuck after she placed a collection of utensils inside it.

She pulled.

Nothing.

She pulled harder.

The drawer made a noise suggesting that it had begun reconsidering its existence.

"Come on."

She pulled again.

The drawer opened suddenly.

Three spoons fell onto the floor.

Aria stared at them.

"Excellent. A successful negotiation."

Her phone rang.

She looked at the screen.

**Marcus Vale.**

She answered.

"Good morning."

"Good morning, Miss Sterling. I hope I'm not disturbing you."

"You're not. I'm currently losing an argument with furniture."

There was a brief silence.

"I see."

"Do you?"

"Not entirely."

"Wise answer."

Marcus cleared his throat.

"I am calling regarding your possible visit to the Vesper residence."

Aria picked up the spoons.

"Is something wrong?"

"No. Your family would like to know whether you would be comfortable meeting them."

Aria placed the spoons on the counter.

"All of them?"

"Your parents, grandparents, and brothers."

"All at once?"

"If that is your preference."

She thought about it.

Meeting one stranger was manageable.

Meeting eight strangers who believed they were her family was a different problem.

"Could I meet my parents first?"

"Of course."

"And maybe my grandparents?"

"Certainly."

"Not the brothers?"

Marcus paused.

"I can arrange that."

"Good."

She picked up a screwdriver.

"How far away do they live?"

"The Vesper Estate is approximately forty minutes from your apartment."

Aria stopped.

"Forty minutes?"

"Yes."

"Is it a normal house?"

Marcus considered his answer.

"That depends on your definition of normal."

Aria sighed.

"I knew it."

**9:03 a.m. — Vesper Estate**

Marcus entered Octavian's private study.

The old patriarch was reading a report.

"She wants to meet her parents and grandparents first," Marcus said.

Octavian lowered the report.

"Only them?"

"Yes, Chairman."

Helena, who was sitting nearby, immediately stood.

"When?"

"She has not suggested a date yet."

"Ask her."

"Madam, she may need time."

Helena stopped.

She understood.

She had imagined this meeting a thousand times.

In some versions, her daughter ran into her arms.

In others, she cried.

In the worst versions, she never came home.

Reality had offered something far more difficult.

A daughter who had grown up without knowing her.

A daughter who was not ready to trust her.

"You're right," Helena said quietly. "We mustn't rush her."

Isolde approached and placed a hand on her shoulder.

"She will come when she is ready."

Octavian nodded.

"Prepare the residence, but do not turn it into a ceremony."

Helena looked at him.

"How should I dress?"

"Comfortably."

She frowned.

"Comfortably?"

"She is coming to meet her mother, not inspect a diplomatic delegation."

Isolde smiled.

"He's right."

Octavian looked mildly offended.

"I am frequently right."

Isolde took a sip of tea.

"Not frequently enough to justify announcing it."

**10:17 a.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria finally defeated the drawer.

The victory lasted approximately three minutes.

Her phone rang again.

This time, it was Sebastian.

She stared at the screen.

Then answered.

"What?"

"Good morning to you too."

"Is there a reason you're calling?"

"Several."

"Pick the shortest."

Sebastian laughed.

"You're impossible."

"That's not a reason."

He paused.

"Are you all right?"

Aria stopped moving.

The question was simple.

It was also the first thing he had said that didn't sound like an accusation.

"I'm fine."

"Really?"

"Yes."

"Where are you staying?"

"In an apartment."

"Which one?"

"One with walls."

"Aria."

"Sebastian."

He sighed.

"Father wants to know if you need anything."

Aria looked around her apartment.

The boxes were unpacked.

The kitchen worked, mostly.

The bedroom had a bed.

She had food.

She had money.

She was fine.

"Tell him I don't need anything."

"Are you sure?"

"Yes."

"You're still angry."

Aria leaned against the counter.

"Should I be happy?"

He went quiet.

"That's not what I meant."

"I know."

She looked through the window.

"Sebastian, I don't want to argue with you."

"Neither do I."

"Then don't."

Another pause.

"All right."

She was about to end the call when he spoke again.

"Are you going to meet your biological family?"

Aria frowned.

"How do you know about that?"

"I have ears."

"Congratulations."

"Aria."

"What?"

"Be careful."

She looked at the phone.

"Why?"

"I don't know them."

"Neither do I."

"Exactly."

For once, he sounded genuinely concerned.

Aria considered saying something kinder.

Instead, she said:

"I'll manage."

Then she ended the call.

**11:06 a.m. — Vesper Estate**

The family had received an unexpected visitor.

Felix stood outside the main study holding a large cardboard box.

Maximilian looked at it.

"What is that?"

"A welcome gift."

"What's inside?"

"Several things."

"Define several."

Felix opened the box.

Inside were two laptops, a tablet, a phone, a portable charger, wireless headphones, a smart watch, and a small home security device.

Cassian looked inside.

"Did you buy an electronics shop?"

"No. These are the latest models."

Damian picked up the security device.

"Why is this included?"

"Her apartment should have better protection."

"Did she ask for this?"

Felix hesitated.

"No."

"Then why did you buy it?"

"Because it's useful."

Maximilian rubbed his forehead.

"We are not giving her an entire technology department."

Felix looked disappointed.

"I already configured everything."

Cassian lifted the headphones.

"Can I have these?"

"No."

"Then why are they in the box?"

"They're for Aria."

Cassian put them back.

"Unfortunate."

Octavian entered the room.

"What is happening?"

Maximilian pointed toward the box.

"Felix has purchased an electronics store."

"It's a gift," Felix protested.

Octavian looked at the devices.

Then at Felix.

"Did she ask for them?"

"No."

"Then put them away."

Felix's shoulders dropped.

Octavian turned toward Helena.

"Have we heard from her?"

"Not yet."

"Good. Give her time."

Cassian glanced at the box.

"Should we at least send something small?"

Octavian looked at him.

"Define small."

Cassian thought for a moment.

"A car?"

"No."

"An apartment?"

"No."

"A private island?"

"Absolutely not."

Cassian shrugged.

"You're making this difficult."

**12:32 p.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria was unhappy.

Her new wardrobe had arrived.

The staff had arranged everything according to her instructions.

The jewellery collection had been presented.

Her bedroom had been redecorated.

Yet something was missing.

Attention.

She sat in front of the mirror while a stylist adjusted her hair.

"Do you think this looks good?" she asked.

"Beautiful, Miss Victoria."

Victoria studied her reflection.

"Only beautiful?"

The stylist froze.

"Exceptionally beautiful."

"Better."

She picked up her phone.

A photograph of her new bedroom had received hundreds of reactions.

She smiled.

Then she noticed a message from Alexander.

**Alexander:** Father wants you downstairs.

Victoria sighed.

"Of course."

She stood.

"Is he free now?"

The stylist nodded.

Victoria walked downstairs.

Richard was waiting in the study.

"Sit down."

She sat opposite him.

"What is it, Father?"

"Have you spoken to Aria?"

Victoria's expression tightened.

"No."

"Good."

Victoria blinked.

"Good?"

"I don't want unnecessary arguments while the legal arrangements are being completed."

Victoria nodded slowly.

"Of course."

Richard placed a document on the desk.

"The family lawyers are reviewing the inheritance structure."

Victoria's eyes moved toward the papers.

"Does that mean everything will be transferred to me?"

"That is not how inheritance works."

"Then how does it work?"

Richard sighed.

"We will discuss it when the legal position is clear."

Victoria smiled.

She had expected this.

The Sterling fortune was enormous.

The family name carried influence.

She had finally returned to the life she believed she deserved.

And she intended to enjoy every part of it.

**1:18 p.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria was eating noodles when a message arrived.

**Marcus Vale:** Miss Sterling, your parents would be grateful to meet you. Would tomorrow afternoon suit you? We can arrange transportation if needed.

Aria looked at the message.

Tomorrow.

That was soon.

She put down her fork.

Her biological parents.

She wondered what they looked like.

Whether they were kind.

Whether they would ask questions.

Whether they expected her to call them Mother and Father immediately.

She didn't know.

She typed:

**Aria:** Tomorrow is fine. I'll come by myself.

Marcus replied almost instantly.

**Marcus:** Of course. We will send you the address and confirm the arrangements.

Aria put her phone down.

Then she returned to her noodles.

A moment later, she picked up the phone again.

**Aria:** One question. Should I bring anything?

Marcus read the message.

He stared at it.

Then he walked directly into Octavian's study.

"Chairman."

Octavian looked up.

"What is it?"

"Miss Sterling has agreed to visit tomorrow."

Helena immediately stood.

"Tomorrow?"

"Yes, Madam."

Octavian smiled.

"Good."

Marcus continued.

"She also asked whether she should bring anything."

Helena looked surprised.

"Bring something?"

"Yes."

Isolde smiled.

"Tell her no."

Marcus nodded.

"Anything else?"

Helena thought for a moment.

"Ask her what she likes to eat."

Marcus blinked.

"What she likes?"

"Yes. I want to prepare something for her."

Octavian nodded.

"Make sure the kitchen knows."

Marcus left.

Helena remained standing.

"What if she doesn't like the food?"

Isolde took her hand.

"Then we make something else."

"What if she doesn't like the house?"

"Then she doesn't have to stay."

"What if she doesn't like us?"

Isolde looked at her gently.

"Then we give her time to know us."

Helena lowered her eyes.

Twenty years of waiting had not prepared her for the simplest part.

Being a mother to a daughter who did not know her.

**2:04 p.m. — Vesper Estate, Family Kitchen**

The head chef was having a crisis.

Not because the kitchen had run out of ingredients.

Not because the staff had made a mistake.

Because the Chairman's wife had asked for a menu based on the preferences of a young woman nobody in the household had ever met.

"What does she like?" the chef asked.

Helena looked at Marcus.

Marcus looked at his notes.

"She ordered grilled chicken, rice, and vegetables yesterday."

The chef nodded.

"Good. We can prepare that."

"She also likes biscuits."

Helena brightened.

"What kind?"

Marcus checked the report.

"Homemade biscuits."

"That's not a kind."

"I don't have further information."

The chef looked troubled.

"Sweet or savoury?"

Marcus stared at the report.

"I don't know."

The chef slowly put down his pen.

"We are preparing a welcome dinner for the missing Vesper heir, and we don't know whether she likes sweet biscuits?"

Helena looked at him.

"Then make both."

The chef nodded immediately.

"Yes, Madam."

"And simple food."

"Certainly."

"Nothing too extravagant."

"Of course."

Helena considered this.

"Actually, perhaps we should prepare several options."

The chef's expression changed.

"How many?"

Helena looked at Isolde.

Isolde smiled.

"Let her choose."

The chef took a deep breath.

"Understood."

He turned toward his staff.

"Prepare the chicken. Rice. Vegetables. Two kinds of biscuits. And a few additional dishes."

One assistant raised a hand.

"How many additional dishes?"

The chef looked toward the ceiling.

"Enough."

**8:47 p.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria had finished unpacking.

The apartment looked more like a home now.

She had placed her books on the shelf, arranged her clothes, and put the family photograph inside a drawer.

She was sitting on the sofa when her phone rang.

Unknown number.

She answered.

"Hello?"

A woman's voice came through.

"Aria?"

Aria sat upright.

"Yes?"

There was a pause.

"My name is Helena."

Aria's fingers tightened around the phone.

"Hello."

"I'm... I'm your mother."

Neither spoke for several seconds.

Helena had rehearsed this conversation all afternoon.

She had imagined saying something warm.

Something comforting.

Something that would make the distance of twenty years disappear.

But now that she was speaking to her daughter, every prepared sentence had vanished.

"I'm sorry," Helena finally said.

Aria frowned.

"For what?"

"For not finding you sooner."

Aria looked down.

"I don't think you need to apologise for something you didn't know about."

Helena's breath caught.

"Thank you."

Another silence.

Then Helena asked:

"Will you come tomorrow?"

"Yes."

"Good."

Her voice trembled.

"I'll be waiting."

Aria swallowed.

"All right."

"Goodnight, Aria."

"Goodnight."

The call ended.

Aria remained on the sofa.

She looked at the phone.

Then at the clock.

She had just spoken to the woman who had given birth to her.

It had lasted less than two minutes.

And somehow, it felt longer than the previous twenty years.

**Vesper Estate — 8:52 p.m.**

Helena stood in the hallway holding her phone.

Octavian approached.

"Did you speak to her?"

Helena nodded.

"Yes."

"How was it?"

She smiled.

"She said I didn't need to apologise."

Octavian looked at her.

"That sounds like her."

"You haven't met her."

"No."

He glanced toward the photograph on the wall.

"But I have spent twenty years hoping she would be kind."

Helena looked down at her phone.

"She is."

Octavian gently touched her shoulder.

"Tomorrow, we meet her."

**Meanwhile — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria was sitting in her bedroom when her phone rang.

An unfamiliar woman spoke.

"Miss Victoria Sterling?"

"Yes. Who is this?"

"My name is Evelyn Ashford. I represent the Vesper family."

Victoria sat up.

"The Vesper family?"

"Yes. We would like to clarify a matter concerning the recent hospital investigation."

Victoria smiled.

Finally.

Someone important.

"Of course. What would you like to discuss?"

There was a pause.

"We would prefer to speak in person."

Victoria glanced at her reflection in the mirror.

"Tomorrow?"

"If convenient."

"Certainly."

She ended the call and smiled.

The Vespers.

She had heard the name before.

A powerful international family.

Perhaps they were connected to her biological parents.

Perhaps they wanted to welcome her.

Victoria stood and opened her wardrobe.

She would need something appropriate.

Something impressive.

Something that made it immediately clear that she belonged among powerful people.

She had no idea that the invitation had nothing to do with her.

And tomorrow, she would learn just how little the world revolved around her.

**End of Chapter 3**
