# **THE WRONG HEIRESS**

## **Chapter 4 — The Welcome Dinner Disaster**

**Vesper Estate — 9:30 a.m.**

The Vesper family had prepared for a guest.

Unfortunately, they had prepared for the wrong kind of guest.

The main dining hall had been decorated with fresh flowers. The long table had been polished. The kitchen had prepared enough food for a diplomatic reception.

And eight members of the household were behaving as though a head of state were arriving.

"Why are there twenty-four chairs?" Maximilian asked.

The head butler stood beside the table.

"Madam requested additional seating."

"Why?"

"In case Miss Aria wishes to invite anyone."

Maximilian looked at the table.

"She has never visited this house."

"Yes, sir."

"She doesn't know anyone here."

"Yes, sir."

"And you prepared twenty-four chairs?"

"Yes, sir."

Maximilian stared at him.

The butler remained perfectly composed.

Cassian entered, took one look at the table, and whistled.

"Impressive. Are we expecting the entire government?"

"No," Maximilian replied.

"Then why does this look like a peace treaty?"

"Ask Mother."

Cassian examined the arrangements.

"Perhaps we should add a red carpet."

"Don't."

"An orchestra?"

"Absolutely not."

"A marching band?"

Maximilian turned toward him.

"Would you like to spend the afternoon in the security office?"

Cassian smiled.

"Not particularly."

At the far end of the hall, Helena was inspecting the flowers.

"These are too formal."

The florist looked alarmed.

"Madam?"

"Could you make the arrangements simpler?"

"Certainly."

"And please remove the enormous arrangement near the entrance."

"Of course."

Helena looked at the flowers again.

"Actually, leave those."

The florist nodded.

Then she changed her mind.

"Wait. No. Remove them."

The florist looked at the butler.

The butler looked at the ceiling.

The butler had served the Vesper family for thirty-one years.

He had survived corporate wars, political scandals, assassination attempts, and three generations of Vesper family disagreements.

He was beginning to suspect that none of those experiences had prepared him for a mother decorating a dining room.

**10:15 a.m. — Aria's Apartment**

Aria stood in front of her wardrobe.

She owned several shirts.

Two dresses.

One jacket.

A collection of trousers.

And absolutely nothing that looked appropriate for visiting an international dynasty.

She picked up a black dress.

Looked at it.

Put it back.

She picked up a blue shirt.

Looked at it.

Put it back.

Finally, she chose a simple white blouse and dark trousers.

"Good enough."

Her phone rang.

Marcus.

"Good morning, Miss Sterling."

"Good morning."

"I wanted to confirm that today's visit is still convenient."

"Yes."

"Would you like transportation arranged?"

"No, thank you. I'll take a taxi."

"Certainly."

A short pause followed.

"Miss Sterling?"

"Yes?"

"Your family is looking forward to meeting you."

Aria adjusted her sleeve.

"That's nice."

"Very much."

"Marcus?"

"Yes?"

"Please tell them not to prepare anything special."

Marcus looked toward the dining hall.

He could see twenty-four chairs.

He could see the flowers.

He could see a kitchen staff member carrying a tray containing enough biscuits to feed a small village.

"Of course," he said.

Aria ended the call.

Marcus slowly lowered his phone.

He turned toward the head butler.

"Remove half the chairs."

**11:42 a.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria had spent nearly two hours choosing an outfit.

She finally settled on a cream-coloured designer dress, matching heels, and a necklace Eleanor had given her.

She looked at herself in the mirror.

Perfect.

Her phone displayed the Vesper family's address.

A private estate.

A powerful family.

And an invitation to meet them.

She smiled.

"Finally."

A knock sounded.

Eleanor entered.

"Victoria, darling, are you ready?"

"Yes, Mother."

"Where are you going?"

"To meet the Vespers."

Eleanor looked surprised.

"The Vespers?"

"They contacted me yesterday."

Richard entered behind Eleanor.

"What did they want?"

"To discuss the hospital investigation."

Richard frowned.

"Why would they contact you?"

Victoria shrugged.

"Perhaps they want to clarify my biological family's circumstances."

Richard looked at Eleanor.

"Did you know about this?"

"No."

Victoria picked up her handbag.

"Don't worry, Father. I'll handle it."

Richard did not look convinced.

"Be careful what you say."

Victoria smiled.

"Father, I know how to speak to important people."

She walked out.

Richard watched her leave.

"She has been here less than three days."

Eleanor smiled faintly.

"She's excited."

Richard looked toward the door.

"I hope she understands that the Vespers are not people to impress with expensive clothes."

**12:18 p.m. — Vesper Estate**

Aria's taxi stopped outside a pair of enormous gates.

She leaned forward.

"Is this the address?"

The driver checked his navigation.

"Yes, madam."

Aria looked through the window.

The property extended beyond the gates. Trees lined a long private driveway. Security cameras were positioned discreetly along the entrance.

A uniformed guard approached the taxi.

"May I know your name?"

"Aria Sterling."

The guard checked his tablet.

His expression changed.

He immediately straightened.

"Welcome, Miss."

The gates opened.

The taxi drove forward.

Aria watched the estate pass by.

There were gardens.

Fountains.

A large building that appeared to have several wings.

And another building behind it.

She looked at the driver.

"Is this a hotel?"

The driver glanced at her.

"No, madam."

"Right."

The taxi stopped near the main entrance.

A man in a dark suit was waiting.

Marcus.

He approached and opened the door.

"Welcome, Miss Sterling."

Aria stepped out.

She looked at the building.

Then at Marcus.

"How many people live here?"

Marcus paused.

"Several members of the family and household staff."

"That's not a number."

"No, Miss."

She looked toward the entrance.

"Do they all eat together?"

"Not usually."

"Good. I was worried about the dishes."

Marcus smiled.

"Please come inside."

**12:24 p.m. — The Vesper Residence**

Helena was waiting near the entrance.

Beside her stood Lucian.

Octavian and Isolde were a little farther back.

When Aria entered, Helena immediately recognised her.

Not because of the photograph.

Because of the eyes.

She had spent twenty years wondering whether her daughter would inherit her features.

Now she was standing a few steps away.

"Aria."

Aria stopped.

"Hello."

Helena approached slowly.

For a moment, she seemed unsure whether to embrace her.

Then she asked quietly:

"May I?"

Aria understood.

She hesitated.

Then nodded.

Helena wrapped her arms around her daughter.

It was a gentle embrace.

Not demanding.

Not possessive.

Just a mother holding the child she had lost.

Aria stood still for a moment.

Then she returned the embrace.

Helena closed her eyes.

Lucian looked away.

Octavian remained silent.

Even Marcus lowered his gaze.

When they separated, Helena wiped her eyes.

"I'm sorry."

Aria gave a small smile.

"You've already apologised."

Helena laughed softly.

"Yes. I suppose I have."

Lucian stepped forward.

"I'm Lucian."

Aria looked at him.

Her biological father.

He appeared intimidating, even standing still.

"Hello."

He offered his hand.

She shook it.

He looked at their joined hands as if he had just received something extraordinarily valuable.

"I'm glad you're here."

Aria nodded.

"Thank you for inviting me."

Octavian approached next.

"Come here, child."

Aria looked at him.

"You're my grandfather?"

"Yes."

"Octavian Vesper?"

"Indeed."

She studied his face.

"You're older than I expected."

Everyone went still.

Octavian blinked.

Then he laughed.

A deep, unexpected laugh.

"Well, I can hardly argue with that."

Isolde approached.

"And I'm Isolde."

Aria smiled.

"Hello, Grandmother."

Isolde took her hands.

"Welcome home, dear."

Aria looked around the enormous entrance hall.

"Thank you. It's a very big home."

Cassian, standing near the staircase, whispered to Felix:

"She's not wrong."

Felix nodded seriously.

"The estate covers several hundred acres."

Cassian closed his eyes.

"Please don't."

**12:41 p.m. — The Dining Hall**

Aria stopped at the entrance.

She looked at the table.

Then at Helena.

Then back at the table.

"Is this for lunch?"

Helena smiled nervously.

"Yes."

Aria counted the chairs.

There were still sixteen.

"How many people are eating?"

"Only the family."

Aria looked toward the table.

"How many people are in the family?"

Cassian appeared behind her.

"An excellent question. We're still negotiating the definition."

Maximilian gave him a warning look.

Cassian ignored it.

Helena guided Aria toward her seat.

"Please sit wherever you're comfortable."

Aria chose a chair near her mother.

A servant began serving the food.

There was chicken, rice, vegetables, soup, several side dishes, and an assortment of desserts.

Aria stared.

"Did you prepare all this?"

Helena nodded.

"We wanted you to have choices."

Aria looked at the food.

Then at the kitchen entrance.

"How many people cooked this?"

"Several."

She considered the amount of food.

"I hope you have a lot of hungry staff."

Isolde laughed.

Octavian smiled.

"Don't worry about the food."

"Good. Because I was beginning to feel responsible."

The first few minutes passed quietly.

Helena asked about Aria's apartment.

Lucian asked about her education.

Octavian asked whether she had been treated well at the Sterling household.

Aria answered honestly.

"They gave me a comfortable life."

"Did they treat you kindly?" Helena asked.

Aria considered the question.

"Most of the time."

"And recently?"

Aria lowered her fork.

"Recently, things changed."

Nobody interrupted.

"They found out I wasn't their biological daughter. Then they found Victoria."

Lucian's expression tightened.

"And they asked you to leave."

"Yes."

Octavian's hand rested against the table.

"Did they give you a choice?"

"Not really."

"Do you want us to intervene?"

Aria looked at him.

"Why?"

"Because what happened to you was wrong."

She shook her head.

"I don't want to start a fight."

Octavian watched her carefully.

"Then we won't start one."

Aria nodded.

"Thank you."

A moment later, she took another bite.

"By the way, the chicken is very good."

Helena smiled.

"Would you like more?"

"Yes, please."

The entire room seemed to relax.

**1:26 p.m. — The Same Dining Hall**

Aria had finished her lunch.

She was drinking tea while Helena asked about her favourite foods.

Then the conversation turned to the hospital records.

Octavian placed a folder on the table.

"We would like to show you something."

Aria put down her cup.

"All right."

He opened the folder.

Inside were copies of the original hospital records, genetic reports, and several documents relating to the investigation.

Aria looked through them.

"These are the original records?"

"Some are copies," Lucian explained. "The originals are secured."

She examined the genetic report.

Three independent laboratory results.

All matching.

She looked at the date.

Then at the names.

"There's no mistake?"

"No," Lucian answered.

Aria slowly closed the folder.

"So I really am a Vesper."

Helena reached across the table.

"Yes, darling."

Aria looked at her.

"All right."

Octavian leaned forward.

"There's something else."

Aria waited.

"We believe your disappearance was deliberate."

She looked down at the folder.

"Marcus mentioned that."

"We are investigating the people involved."

"Do you think they wanted to hurt me?"

Octavian's expression became serious.

"We don't know."

Aria nodded.

"Then find out."

The old patriarch studied her.

"Is that what you want?"

"Yes. I would like to know why someone switched us."

Octavian nodded.

"We will find the truth."

Aria looked at the documents again.

"Please don't hide anything from me."

"We won't."

"Even if it's unpleasant?"

"Even then."

She nodded.

"Good."

**1:48 p.m. — The Front Gate**

A black luxury car stopped outside the Vesper estate.

Victoria stepped out.

She adjusted her dress and checked her reflection in her phone.

The security guard approached.

"Good afternoon. May I help you?"

"I'm here to meet the Vesper family."

"Do you have an appointment?"

"Yes."

"May I have your name?"

"Victoria Sterling."

The guard checked his tablet.

He frowned.

"I'm afraid I don't see your name."

Victoria smiled.

"Perhaps you should check again."

He did.

Nothing.

"Miss, I don't have authorisation to let you in."

Victoria's smile disappeared.

"Do you know who I am?"

The guard remained professional.

"No, Miss. That's why I'm asking."

Victoria stared at him.

"I am Richard Sterling's daughter."

"Understood."

"I am also the biological daughter of the Sterling family."

The guard looked at the tablet again.

"Miss, I still don't have an appointment under your name."

Victoria's cheeks flushed.

"Call your manager."

The guard contacted the security office.

A few minutes later, Marcus received the call.

"Sir, there is a visitor at the gate. Victoria Sterling. She claims she has an appointment."

Marcus looked at the schedule.

"She does not."

"Shall we ask her to leave?"

Marcus paused.

"Tell her that the Vesper family has no meeting scheduled with her."

"Understood."

Victoria heard the response.

Her expression changed.

"No meeting?"

The guard nodded.

"That is correct, Miss."

"But I was contacted!"

"By whom?"

"Evelyn Ashford."

The guard checked again.

"One moment, please."

He contacted Marcus a second time.

Marcus was silent for several seconds.

Then he understood.

Evelyn had been instructed to contact Victoria Sterling to clarify the hospital investigation.

The invitation had not been for a family visit.

"Tell her that Miss Ashford is available for a meeting at the Vesper Tower offices tomorrow, if she wishes to discuss the investigation."

The guard relayed the message.

Victoria stared at him.

"Tomorrow?"

"Yes, Miss."

"Not here?"

"No, Miss."

Victoria's face tightened.

She looked past the gates toward the estate.

"Who is visiting today?"

The guard did not answer.

"Is it Aria?"

The guard remained silent.

Victoria's expression changed completely.

Of course.

Aria.

The girl who had occupied her place.

The girl who had been raised as a Sterling.

The girl who had supposedly lost everything.

And now she was visiting the Vespers.

Victoria's hands tightened around her handbag.

"Tell Marcus Vale I want to speak with him."

"I'm afraid he is occupied."

"Then tell him I will wait."

The guard glanced toward the security office.

He had been instructed not to create unnecessary trouble.

He had not been instructed to entertain visitors who refused to leave.

"Miss, I must ask you to step away from the entrance."

Victoria stared at him.

Then she turned sharply and walked back to her car.

As the vehicle pulled away, she took out her phone.

She called Richard.

"Father?"

"What is it?"

"I need to speak to you."

"About what?"

"Aria."

Richard went quiet.

"What happened?"

Victoria looked back at the gates.

"She's meeting the Vespers."

**2:07 p.m. — Vesper Estate**

Aria stood near the entrance, preparing to leave.

Helena held her hand.

"Would you like to stay longer?"

"Thank you, but I have some things to do."

"Of course."

Lucian stepped forward.

"Would you like a car?"

Aria smiled.

"I'll take a taxi."

Lucian looked at Marcus.

Marcus looked at Lucian.

Neither said anything.

Octavian approached.

"Then we won't insist."

Aria nodded.

"Thank you for lunch."

Helena hugged her again.

"Can we see you soon?"

"Yes. We can arrange something."

Helena smiled.

"Good."

As Aria walked toward the entrance, Octavian spoke.

"Aria."

She turned.

"Yes?"

"You are welcome here whenever you wish."

Aria looked at him.

Then at Helena.

Then Lucian.

"Thank you, Grandfather."

Octavian smiled.

She left.

For a few moments, nobody moved.

Then Helena quietly said:

"She called me Mother."

Lucian looked at her.

"She did."

Isolde smiled.

"That is a beginning."

**3:15 p.m. — Vesper Estate, Private Study**

Marcus entered Octavian's study.

"Chairman, there was an incident at the entrance."

Octavian looked up.

"What happened?"

"Victoria Sterling arrived without an appointment. She asked to meet the family."

"Did you speak with her?"

"No. I instructed security to offer her a meeting with Miss Ashford at Vesper Tower tomorrow."

Octavian nodded.

"Fine."

Marcus hesitated.

"She appeared to believe she had been invited to the estate."

Octavian's expression hardened.

"Why?"

"She was contacted regarding the hospital investigation. The invitation was limited to a meeting with our legal representative."

Octavian looked toward the window.

"Then make certain the distinction is clear."

"Yes, Chairman."

Marcus turned to leave.

"One more thing," Octavian said.

"Sir?"

"Did Aria notice?"

"No. She was unaware of the incident."

"Good."

Octavian returned to his papers.

"Do not let the Sterlings disturb her peace."

**3:42 p.m. — Sterling Mansion**

Victoria stormed into the study.

Richard stood up.

"What happened?"

"They refused to let me inside."

Richard frowned.

"Who?"

"The Vespers."

"Did you have an appointment?"

"I was invited!"

"By whom?"

"Someone named Evelyn."

Richard stared at her.

"Did she invite you to meet the family?"

Victoria hesitated.

"She said they wanted to discuss the hospital investigation."

Richard slowly sat down.

"That isn't the same thing."

Victoria's expression darkened.

"Aria was there."

Richard looked up.

"How do you know?"

"I saw her taxi leaving."

Silence.

Victoria folded her arms.

"So she gets to meet them, and I get sent to an office?"

Richard had no immediate answer.

Victoria lifted her chin.

"This is ridiculous. They should understand that I am the real daughter."

Richard looked at her carefully.

"Victoria, the Vespers are not the Sterlings. You cannot demand their attention."

Victoria stared at him.

Then she smiled.

"Perhaps they simply haven't realised who I am yet."

Richard closed his eyes.

For the first time since Victoria's return, he wondered whether welcoming her home had been the beginning of a much larger problem.

**End of Chapter 4**
