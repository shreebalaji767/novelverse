# **Chapter 55: The Former Empress Has Been Invited to Inspect the Imperial Gardens. The Marquis's Eldest Daughter Has Been Invited to Inspect a Production Report. One Garden Has Too Many Gardeners. One Workshop Has Too Many Opinions About the Correct Way to Fold Silk.**

**18th Day of the Fifth Month, 18th Year of Yonghe.**



The Imperial Household Administration had prepared a garden report.



The Emperor had not requested one.



The report had nevertheless arrived on his desk.



Gao Shun placed it beside the morning correspondence.



The Emperor glanced at the heading.



"Why is this here?"



Gao Shun bowed.



"The gardeners have submitted their maintenance summary before the visit on the twenty-first."



"Who requested it?"



"No one specifically, Your Majesty."



"Then why have they submitted it?"



Gao Shun considered the answer.



"They may have wished to demonstrate that the gardens are prepared."



The Emperor opened the report.



It contained descriptions of repaired paths, trimmed hedges, cleaned ponds, and replaced flower beds.



One paragraph explained that the eastern pavilion's wooden railing had been polished.



Another stated that the western path had been swept twice.



A third mentioned that the gardeners had moved several pots to improve the view.



The Emperor read the report to the end.



"Have they been asked to change the garden for the visit?"



"No, Your Majesty."



"Then why have they moved the pots?"



Gao Shun had no answer.



The Emperor put down the report.



"Tell them to leave the garden as it is unless something requires repair."



"Yes, Your Majesty."



"And tell the gardeners not to prepare another report unless there is an actual problem."



Gao Shun accepted the document.



The Emperor looked at the garden report once more.



He had invited Yuru to take a walk.



Somehow, the invitation had become a maintenance project.

At her temporary residence, Yuru was selecting a pair of comfortable shoes.



Fang Ru had prepared two pairs.



One was suitable for ordinary walking.



The other was more formal.



Yuru chose the first.



"Will the Empress Dowager attend?" she asked.



"Yes. The palace sent confirmation this morning."



"Then we should arrive at the agreed time."



Fang Ru nodded.



"Should we bring anything?"



"No. It is a garden visit."



Fang Ru smiled.



"His Majesty may have arranged refreshments."



"That is his responsibility."



Yuru put on the shoes.



Fang Ru looked at her.



"You sound rather pleased."



"I am."



Yuru glanced at the invitation.



She had spent many years walking through those gardens while discussing palace matters.



She remembered the ponds, the old trees, the stone bridges, and the narrow paths behind the western pavilion.



She was looking forward to seeing them without carrying a list of pending decisions.

At the eastern market, Mingzhao was reviewing the production report.



The report had been prepared by Lin Shuyun.



It contained the quantity of material prepared, the quantity passed through initial inspection, the amount still under examination, and the training records of the four additional workers.



Mingzhao examined the figures.



The totals matched.



The production had not fallen outside the revised schedule.



The two trainees had improved, though neither was ready to work independently.



One had become more careful.



The other had become slower.



Lin sat across from her.



"Is there anything else?"



Mingzhao turned to the final page.



"Who approved this change?"



Lin looked at the note.



"The senior finisher."



"Was she authorized to change the folding method?"



"She believed it would reduce handling damage."



"Was the buyer's specification checked?"



Lin paused.



"Not yet."



Mingzhao placed the report down.



"Then the change cannot be adopted for the order until we confirm it does not affect the agreed finish."



Lin sighed.



"The senior finisher has worked with silk for twenty years."



"That is useful experience. It does not replace the buyer's specifications."



Lin called for the senior finisher.



The woman arrived shortly afterwards.



She was experienced, confident, and clearly displeased that her decision had been questioned.



"I changed the method because the material was slipping during preparation," she explained.



"Did the new method improve the result?" Mingzhao asked.



"Yes."



"Did you compare it with the buyer's sample?"



"No."



"Then we should compare it before adopting the change."



The senior finisher frowned.



"I know how silk should be handled."



Mingzhao remained calm.



"I do not doubt your experience. But this order has agreed specifications. We cannot change them without checking."



Lin looked between the two women.



The senior finisher finally nodded.



"Very well. I will prepare a comparison."



Mingzhao returned to the report.



"Record both methods and their results."



The senior finisher looked mildly offended.



"Must everything be written down?"



Mingzhao answered without hesitation.



"Anything that changes the production method should be."



The woman left.



Lin rubbed her forehead.



"You have managed to offend the most experienced person in the workshop."



"I have asked her to document a change."



"She does not like being questioned."



"Then she should provide a result that answers the question."



Lin stared at Mingzhao.



After a moment, she laughed.



"Sometimes I think you would ask a thunderstorm to provide a written explanation."



"If it damaged the roof, yes."

At the Marquisate, Shen Mingyuan received the tenant's signed temporary cultivation agreement.



The tenant had read the terms and accepted them.



The estate retained a signed copy.



The tenant received another.



The arrangement would remain in force for the stated period, subject to the agreed notice conditions.



Mingyuan checked the signatures.



Steward Zhou stood beside him.



"That matter is now recorded."



Mingyuan nodded.



"Update the property schedule."



"Should it be listed as leased?"



Mingyuan considered the wording.



"No. The previous lease has expired. Record the current temporary cultivation agreement separately."



Steward Zhou smiled faintly.



"You have learned to be careful with headings."



Mingyuan looked at him.



"I have learned that a wrong heading can create another meeting."



The steward laughed.



Mingyuan continued reviewing the records.



There were still matters to resolve.



The northern warehouse grain discrepancy remained open.



The duplicate transport charge lacked sufficient supporting evidence.



The southern building repair estimate required a condition report.



The agricultural holdings had not been fully valued.



The family settlement was nowhere near complete.



But one matter had been clarified.



That was enough for the morning.

Mingxiu was visiting the southern building with the steward.



The roof was in better condition than the contractor's first report had suggested.



Several tiles needed replacement.



Others could be repaired.



The building's interior remained dry.



Mingxiu examined the beams and the ceiling.



"Does the roof need immediate work?"



The steward looked at the damaged section.



"It should be repaired before the heavier summer rains."



"Then we should schedule it before the next maintenance period becomes crowded."



She made a note.



"Use the corrected estimate, but have the contractor report any additional damage before replacing more tiles."



The steward nodded.



Mingxiu looked at the building.



She had once thought that estate management meant knowing what needed to be done.



She was beginning to understand that it also meant knowing what did not need to be done immediately.

At the palace, the Empress Dowager received a message from the Emperor.



He had asked her to join the garden visit.



She read the message and set it aside.



An attendant approached.



"Your Majesty, shall the western pavilion be prepared?"



"Clean it."



"Should the musicians be arranged?"



"No."



"Refreshments?"



"Tea and fruit."



The attendant bowed.



The Dowager looked towards the window.



"Has the former Empress confirmed?"



"Yes, Your Majesty."



"Then there is no need to make the visit complicated."



The attendant withdrew.



The Dowager smiled faintly.



She had once considered a gathering incomplete without proper arrangements.



Now she was discovering that some gatherings were improved by having fewer arrangements.

The Emperor, meanwhile, had received a note from the Third Princess.



Minglan had completed the comparison of the two uncertain library entries.



One book was identified.



The other remained unresolved.



She had found an older catalogue reference, but the surviving description was incomplete.



The Emperor read her note.



He wrote a brief reply praising her careful work.



Then he looked at the accompanying request.



The librarian had asked whether the unidentified entry should be removed from the catalogue.



The Emperor wrote:



*Retain the entry as unresolved. Do not remove it merely because the title has not yet been confirmed.*



He returned the document.



The library catalogue would remain imperfect.



The Emperor was beginning to accept that a record honestly marked uncertain was more useful than one made complete by invention.

In the afternoon, Lin Shuyun brought Mingzhao the comparison of the two folding methods.



The senior finisher had prepared two small samples using the same material.



One followed the original buyer specification.



The other used the revised method.



Mingzhao examined both.



The revised method was faster.



However, it created a slightly different edge appearance.



Lin compared the samples.



"The difference is small."



"It is still a difference."



"Would the buyer reject it?"



"We do not know."



The senior finisher crossed her arms.



"The material is not damaged."



Mingzhao looked at her.



"I agree. But the question is whether it matches the approved sample."



The senior finisher was silent.



Lin examined the original specification.



"The buyer did not state that the folding method itself was mandatory."



"No," Mingzhao said. "But the final appearance was."



The three women compared the samples again.



They agreed to send a description and sample to Zhou Wenqi before adopting the revised method for the main order.



Until then, the workshop would continue using the original process.



The senior finisher was not pleased.



She was, however, willing to follow the decision.



Lin collected the samples.



"You have delayed the new method."



"We have prevented an unapproved change."



"That sounds less pleasant."



"It is more accurate."



Lin shook her head.

The following morning, Yuru received a small note from the Emperor.



It confirmed the time of the garden visit.



It also stated that the paths had been checked and were safe.



Yuru read the note.



Fang Ru asked, "Is everything ready?"



"Apparently."



"Will you bring your walking shoes?"



"I have already chosen them."



Fang Ru smiled.



Yuru folded the note and placed it beside her books.



She was looking forward to the visit.



She was also looking forward to returning without a report.

At the workshop, the two trainees completed another practice session.



The slower worker had improved her speed.



The faster worker had improved her accuracy.



The senior finisher recorded their progress.



Mingzhao examined the entries.



"Good."



Lin looked pleased.



"Are you satisfied?"



"For today's work."



Lin laughed.



"Only today's work?"



"Tomorrow has not happened."



Lin shook her head.



"You are determined to make every day a separate account."



Mingzhao closed the ledger.



"Every day has separate work."

That evening, the palace prepared for the garden visit.



The gardeners cleaned the paths.



The pavilion was inspected.



Tea and fruit were arranged.



No musicians were summoned.



No formal procession was planned.



The Empress Dowager approved the arrangements.



The Emperor approved them as well.



Then he sent a final instruction to Gao Shun.



"Do not bring any documents."



Gao Shun bowed.



"Yes, Your Majesty."



The Emperor looked at him.



"That includes documents that happen to concern the gardens."



Gao Shun hesitated.



"Yes, Your Majesty."



The Emperor returned to his study.



He had learned that a garden visit was not improved by bringing a maintenance report.

The eighteenth day of the fifth month ended with several small decisions.



The palace had agreed to keep the garden visit informal.



The Empress Dowager would attend.



Yuru would bring no documents.



Mingzhao had rejected an unapproved change to the silk finishing method.



The workshop would continue using the agreed process until the buyer reviewed the alternative sample.



Mingyuan had completed the temporary cultivation agreement.



Mingxiu had confirmed that the southern building's roof repairs should be scheduled before the heavier summer rains.



The Crown Prince had begun learning to preserve uncertainty in records rather than conceal it.



The former Empress was preparing to walk through a garden.



The Marquis's eldest daughter was preparing to inspect another production report.



One would soon see flowers.



The other would soon see two samples of folded silk.



Both had discovered that a pleasant appearance was not enough.



It had to match the agreed specification.
