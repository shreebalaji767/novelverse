# Chapter 102 — The Three Containers That Had Never Been Missing

The second correction arrived through the inventory office.

It was less formal than the first.

It was also more irritating.

Seraphina discovered it while reviewing the estate's own procurement ledger.

A copy of the capital's final stock reconciliation had been attached to the determination. The document included the eighteen containers transferred through the Central Reserve Materials Depot.

The original accounting was clear.

Six had been placed in the chemical reference store.

Six had been transferred to the catalyst laboratory.

Four had entered the Department's controlled store.

One had been consumed during a comparison test.

One had been transferred to another research section.

Three of the six catalyst-laboratory containers had later been used during the central assembly repair.

The remaining three were still in the laboratory.

The total was eighteen.

There was no shortage.

There was no missing batch.

Yet Chapter 99's administrative summary had referred to three western depot containers as unresolved.

Seraphina had not seen that wording in the final determination.

She found it in an earlier draft attached to the review papers.

The phrase had survived from a preliminary worksheet.

It was not supported by the physical inventory.

She placed the two documents side by side.

Cedric entered the room.

"What is it?"

"An accounting error."

"New?"

"Old. It has simply survived long enough to become inconvenient."

He approached the table.

Seraphina pointed to the disputed line.

"Here. Three western depot containers."

Cedric read it.

"Where are they?"

"They are not a separate group."

"Then why are they listed?"

"Because the preliminary worksheet described three containers used during the western and central maintenance period. The wording was copied into a later summary without preserving the reference to the original batch."

Cedric examined the inventory table.

"These are the three used in the central assembly repair?"

"Yes."

"And they were already counted among the eighteen?"

"Yes."

"So the summary makes them appear to be an additional three."

"Exactly."

Cedric frowned.

"That is a serious error."

"It would be if the physical stock depended on the summary. Fortunately, it does not."

She turned to a blank sheet.

The correction was simple.

The disputed reference would be withdrawn.

The three containers would remain recorded as part of the original six transferred to the catalyst laboratory, with three used in the central assembly repair and three remaining in laboratory stock.

No western depot stock would be added to the total.

No additional containers would be invented to make the figures agree.

The corrected inventory would read:

| Destination or use | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Chemical reference store | 6 |
| Catalyst laboratory, remaining | 3 |
| Department controlled store | 4 |
| Central assembly repair | 3 |
| Comparison test | 1 |
| Transfer to another research section | 1 |
| **Total** | **18** |

Cedric checked the addition.

"Eighteen."

"Yes."

"Every container accounted for."

"Yes."

"Then why did the preliminary worksheet call them western depot containers?"

Seraphina considered the question.

"Because the maintenance request referred to the western and central repair programme. Someone shortened the description. Someone else copied it. A third person treated the shortened description as a location."

"Three separate mistakes."

"Three separate steps in one administrative mistake."

Cedric looked at the figures again.

"At least nobody has to search another warehouse."

"That would have been a poor use of the afternoon."

The correction was sent to the Secretariat.

This time, the wording was checked against the physical stock register, laboratory receipt book, transfer acknowledgment, and repair consumption record.

The Secretariat issued a corrected index note.

It did not alter the original worksheet.

Instead, it attached a clarification stating that the phrase *three western depot containers* was an inaccurate description of three containers already included in the eighteen-container transfer.

The corrected record preserved the history of the mistake.

It also prevented the mistake from becoming a second inventory.

At the capital's technical stores, the remaining three catalyst-laboratory containers were counted again.

Their seals were intact.

Their identification numbers matched the laboratory register.

The four Department-controlled containers were also confirmed.

The chemical reference store retained its six.

The comparison-test record identified the consumed container.

The research section acknowledged receipt of the transferred container.

The inventory was complete.

A week later, Lucien visited the Veyr estate.

He arrived with a report, two books, and an expression that suggested he had been asked to explain the same thing too many times.

Seraphina looked at the books.

"Are those gifts?"

"Technical manuals."

"That does not answer the question."

"They are reference copies."

"That is a more suspicious answer."

Lucien placed them on the table.

"The Academy has completed its independent reconciliation of the catalyst stock."

"Good."

"All eighteen containers are accounted for."

"I saw the correction."

"The remaining three in the laboratory have been placed under ordinary research inventory controls. They are not reserved for the capital network."

"Also good."

Lucien looked at her.

"You sound disappointed."

"I was hoping the inventory would remain finished."

"It is."

"Then why are you here?"

"Because the Academy wanted to send a formal acknowledgment."

He handed her the report.

It was brief.

The Academy confirmed that the technical materials used during the emergency repair had been properly identified, transferred, consumed, and recorded. No further action was required from House Veyr.

Seraphina read the last paragraph.

"Does this mean the Academy will stop sending me inventory reports?"

Lucien hesitated.

"I cannot promise that."

"Then I cannot promise to read them."

He smiled.

"That sounds fair."

The capital network continued operating under the authorized seven-zone configuration.

The central assembly remained subject to routine inspection.

The reserve continued its balancing function.

The eastern guide and bracket were inspected according to the revised schedule.

The western and southern sections passed their next maintenance checks.

No additional catalyst shortage appeared.

No second emergency developed.

The eighteen containers remained eighteen containers.

And the three that had never been missing were finally allowed to remain where the records said they were.
