WHAT GUIDES US

The principles
behind the platform.

01

Free public reading

Published novels are available to read without subscriptions, advertisements or mandatory reader accounts.

02

Reader-owned shelf

My Library, bookmarks, reading positions and preferences stay in your browser. You can export a backup and restore it later.

03

Stories with context

Series pages bring volumes and chapters together. Character and world encyclopedias provide space for deeper story details.

04

Independent publishing

NovelVerse is maintained as a static website. Published content is added to the repository and deployed through GitHub Pages.

YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE

Private by architecture.

NovelVerse does not use a database, backend, hosted user account system or remote personal-data storage. Public catalogue and chapter files are part of the website. Personal shelf data and reader settings are stored locally in your browser.

That means personal data does not automatically sync between devices. Use the built-in backup tools in My Library to export and import your own data.

Open My Library →

FOR THE PEOPLE WHO MAKE STORIES

Publishing stays independent.

NovelVerse content is maintained as files in the project repository. The Publishing Studio helps prepare and validate a content package in your browser; the publisher reviews the files and commits them to GitHub manually. The site never asks for GitHub passwords or access tokens.
