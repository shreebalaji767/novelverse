OUR PURPOSE

A home for stories.

NovelVerse is an independent light novel publishing and reading platform built around free access, thoughtful design and rich fictional worlds.

Free by design

Stories are publicly accessible without subscriptions, advertisements or mandatory reader accounts.

Reader first

Comfortable reading controls, bookmarks, reading progress and offline access are core platform goals.

Worlds with depth

Character and world encyclopedias will connect the people, places, histories and ideas behind every series.

Independent publishing

Series and chapters are published manually through GitHub and delivered as a static website.
